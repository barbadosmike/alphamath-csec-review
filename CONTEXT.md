# AlphaMath Learning Evidence

This context records learner work, human review, and syllabus-linked evidence without treating activity completion as proof of mastery.

## Language

**Learner**:
A student receiving personalized mathematics instruction, referenced across records by a stable external learner ID.
_Avoid_: User, account, subject

**Intake Submission**:
A dated learner self-assessment containing topic statuses, notes, and practice-question reflections.
_Avoid_: Diagnostic result, mastery test

**Tutorial Attempt**:
The learner's saved answers, confidence responses, hints, and working from one tutorial instrument.
_Avoid_: Tutorial result, lesson completion

**Tutor Review**:
A named human tutor's evaluation of a tutorial attempt, including item outcomes, feedback, and any explicit mastery decisions.
_Avoid_: Auto-grade, AI grade

**Exam Attempt**:
A locked submission from one simulated examination instrument, including responses, timing, and learner review flags.
_Avoid_: Exam result, grade

**Exam Review**:
A named human tutor's item-level marking and overall evaluation of an exam attempt.
_Avoid_: Auto-score

**Evidence Item**:
A tutor-reviewed observation linked to one or more syllabus objectives. Evidence may support a later decision but does not itself establish mastery.
_Avoid_: Mastery, completion

**Mastery Decision**:
An explicit human tutor judgment for a syllabus objective, recorded with reviewer identity and date.
_Avoid_: Completion status, inferred mastery
