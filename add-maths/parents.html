Registration and enrolment

Student registration

Your child's learner record is created by an administrator during enrolment — you do not fill it in yourself. It is keyed to a subject-scoped ID rather than a name, which is what lets the tutor surfaces show progress without showing who the child is.

Registration happens before your account is linked to your child. Your coordinator confirms both IDs with you once enrolment is complete.

Enrolment & payment

Payment details are provided at enrolment through MSE's payment processor. AlphaMath never stores card numbers in its own database — the evidence store holds your child's learning record, not payment credentials.

Fee schedule and processor details are confirmed at enrolment by the coordinator.
