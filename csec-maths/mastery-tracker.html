How “Mastered” is decided (ratified 2026-07-19): ≥ 80% correct across ≥ 4 authentic past-paper items, with correct method where method is scored (Paper 2), and retained across at least one spaced-retrieval checkpoint. Accuracy alone is not mastery — retained accuracy is; a lapse regresses the state. These four columns map to the ratified three-state view: Not started + Not yet → “not-yet” · Learning → “approaching” · Mastered.
